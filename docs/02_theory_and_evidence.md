# Theory and evidence

## Group Field Theory

Group Field Theory is a quantum field theory framework whose fields live on products of group manifolds rather than ordinary spacetime. Field quanta can encode discrete geometric building blocks. Interaction terms specify how these quanta combine.

The key inversion is conceptual:

> The building blocks are not placed inside space. A suitable collective state is interpreted as space.

## Condensate cosmology

GFT condensate cosmology studies states containing many quanta in a common collective configuration. Under symmetry, isotropy, semiclassical, and weak interaction assumptions, collective observables can satisfy equations resembling homogeneous cosmological dynamics.

Published work has shown that:

* Certain condensate states can be interpreted as homogeneous spatial geometry.
* Effective Friedmann dynamics can emerge under controlled approximations.
* Some solutions replace a zero volume singularity with a nonzero minimum volume and a quantum bounce.
* Mean field phase transitions exist in classes of Lorentzian tensorial GFT models.
* Microscopic entanglement structures can produce macroscopic perturbation equations resembling general relativity in restricted regimes.

These are mathematical results inside models. They are not observations of GFT quanta or a measured geometrogenesis event.

## Evidence ladder

### Established

General relativity successfully describes gravitational phenomena as dynamical spacetime geometry across tested regimes. Quantum theory successfully describes microscopic matter and interactions. Neither success proves that spacetime is fundamental.

### Derived in model

Condensate observables can reproduce familiar cosmological equations under explicit assumptions. This establishes internal viability for parts of the research program.

### Interpretation

Calling a phase transition geometrogenesis interprets the geometric phase as emergent spacetime. The physical meaning of the transition depends on how canonical evolution, covariant amplitudes, observables, and relational clocks are connected.

### Speculative

The claims that the Big Bang was geometrogenesis, that ordinary time literally began at that transition, or that our universe is a GFT condensate are currently speculative.

## The problem of time

If time is absent in the pregeometric description, ordinary temporal language becomes dangerous. Saying that geometry formed before time existed can secretly introduce an external clock.

A relational treatment instead expresses one observable as a function of another:

```math
O = O(φ)
```

Here, the scalar field `φ` can act as a clock variable. The formulation does not require a universal external time parameter, but it does require careful interpretation.

## Competing explanations

Emergent spacetime is not unique to GFT. Related questions appear in loop quantum gravity, spin foams, causal sets, causal dynamical triangulations, tensor networks, holography, quantum graphity, and thermodynamic gravity. Any empirical claim must distinguish GFT from these alternatives rather than merely support emergence in general.
