# Limitations and falsifiability

## Primary limitation

The project is an interpretive computational model. It demonstrates that relational emergence is logically and computationally possible. It does not measure quantum gravity or identify the microscopic structure of the universe.

## Known limitations

### Toy dynamics

The phase transition control changes graph structure through simplified browser logic. A physical implementation requires GFT actions, renormalization group flow, and justified continuum observables.

### Projection dependence

Any visual graph requires screen coordinates. The project separates those coordinates from primitive state, but readers may still mistake the projection for physical position.

### Analogy leakage

Water, crystals, crowds, routes, and clocks already exist in spacetime. They explain collective emergence but cannot explain a genuinely nonspatiotemporal substrate without conceptual residue.

### Problem of time

Relational clocks reduce dependence on external time but do not automatically resolve every interpretive and technical problem in quantum gravity.

### Nonuniqueness

Multiple microscopic models can yield similar macroscopic behavior. Recovering general relativity is necessary but may not uniquely identify the underlying theory.

## Conditions for stronger evidence

A stronger GFT case requires:

1. A controlled continuum limit.
2. Recovery of general relativity and quantum field behavior in validated regimes.
3. Stable predictions across justified approximations.
4. At least one distinctive observable that differs from rival theories.
5. Compatibility with cosmological, gravitational wave, black hole, and particle constraints.
6. Independent replication of the numerical pipeline.

## Potential falsifiers

The research program would be weakened if:

* No physically acceptable continuum phase exists.
* Classical spacetime dynamics cannot be recovered without fine tuning.
* Candidate signatures conflict with precision cosmology.
* Relational observables depend on arbitrary clock choice in measurable ways.
* Microscopic assumptions cannot produce robust macroscopic universality.

## Governance requirements

Every public result should label:

* Evidence class
* Mathematical assumptions
* Implemented equations
* Approximation regime
* Parameter values
* Source version
* Known counterexamples
* What the result does not establish

## Acceptance criterion

The project is ready for scientific review only when an independent evaluator can reproduce every displayed metric from a signed experiment receipt and identify which outputs are direct calculations, simplified analogies, or narrative illustrations.
