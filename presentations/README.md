# Presentation

## Before Space, Before Time

The editable PowerPoint deck provides a concise research narrative suitable for technical briefings, public talks, and investor or partner conversations.

### Narrative

1. The inversion: space and time may be outputs.
2. The model: primitive relations without coordinates.
3. The mechanism: collective order and geometrogenesis.
4. The demonstrations: distance, dimension, and relational clocks.
5. The evidence ladder: established, derived, interpreted, unresolved.
6. The RuVector research architecture.
7. The scientific limits and falsifiability gate.
8. The conclusion: possibility proven, physical truth unconfirmed.

The deck cites research sources in its speaker notes and links back to the interactive experience.
