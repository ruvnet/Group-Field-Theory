# Group Field Theory research harness

This harness investigates Group Field Theory, condensate cosmology, geometrogenesis,
and the hypothesis that spacetime is emergent rather than fundamental. Its output is
a research dossier, not a proof of the site's thesis.

## Behavioral rules

- Use the harness's MCP tools (`mcp__group-field-theory-harness__*`) for orchestration.
- Defer destructive operations to the user.
- Prefer peer reviewed papers, preprints from the paper's authors, published datasets,
  and official experiment documentation. Use secondary sources only for context.
- Give every substantive claim exactly one evidence label: `DEFINITION`,
  `MATHEMATICAL`, `NUMERICAL`, `EMPIRICAL`, `INTERPRETATION`, or `SPECULATION`.
- Never turn a mathematical result inside a model into empirical evidence about nature.
  State assumptions, approximation regimes, and whether a result is analytic or numeric.
- Distinguish evidence for internal consistency, recovery of effective spacetime,
  compatibility with observation, and unique experimental confirmation.
- Record disagreements and incompatible assumptions explicitly. Do not average them into
  a consensus or silently select the preferred conclusion.
- Do not claim that Group Field Theory proves spacetime or time does not exist. The maximum
  warranted conclusion is that some models construct familiar spacetime observables from
  non-spatiotemporal degrees of freedom.
- Preserve uncertainty. A missing test, inaccessible source, or unresolved derivation must
  be reported as `UNSUPPORTED` or `UNRESOLVED`, not filled by inference.
